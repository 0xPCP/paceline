Data Use Policy

How Paceline uses platform data to operate and improve the service.

Purpose

Paceline.club uses data to help riders find rides, help clubs manage their online presence, improve safety and reliability, understand whether the platform is useful, and decide what to build next.

Operational Data Use

We use account, club, ride, membership, location, notification, and usage data to provide core Paceline features. This includes ride discovery, maps, weather, membership workflows, dues tracking, club administration, email notifications, feedback review, spam prevention, security monitoring, and support.

Analytics and Product Improvement

We may analyze platform activity to understand club growth, ride participation, rider demand by geography, feature adoption, storage usage, email volume, performance issues, and user experience problems. This helps Paceline decide where to improve the product.

Aggregated and De-identified Data

Paceline may create aggregated or de-identified data that does not reasonably identify an individual user. Paceline may use, disclose, license, or sell this aggregated or de-identified data for business, research, partnership, product, or market analysis purposes.

Identifiable Personal Data

Paceline does not currently sell identifiable personal data. If Paceline later plans to sell or share identifiable personal data, we will update the Privacy Policy and this Data Use Policy and provide legally required choices or consent controls before doing so.

Club Administrator Access

Club administrators may see information needed to operate their clubs, such as membership status, dues status, ride signups, waiver status, emergency contact information where applicable, and club-related activity. Club administrators are expected to use this information only for legitimate club purposes.

Location Data

Location data may be used for maps, nearby search, weather, ride discovery, and product analytics. Users should only provide browser location access when they want location-based features.

Contact

Questions about data use can be sent to privacy@paceline.club.
