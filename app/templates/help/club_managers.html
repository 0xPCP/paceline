Club Manager Guide

Set up your club page, publish rides, and manage your members.

1. Create your club

Sign in, choose Create a Club, and enter the club name, city, state, and zip code. The zip code helps riders find your club on the map and in nearby searches.

Every new club starts hidden. It will not appear in the directory, map, or search results until you unhide it from Settings. This gives you time to fill in the details before riders find you.

During setup you will choose a hosting mode:

  • Full Club: The complete Paceline experience — membership management, waivers, dues tracking, news posts, safety guidelines, and a full public club page. Best for established clubs that want everything managed in one place.
  • Rides Only: A simplified ride calendar with no membership management. Riders can view and sign up for rides without needing to join the club. Great for clubs that already have a website and just want an easy way to publish and share rides.

You can switch modes at any time from Admin → Settings → Hosting Mode. All ride data is preserved when you switch.

In Full Club mode you can also choose whether the club is public or private. Public clubs are easy for anyone to join. Private clubs are better when you want to approve or invite members before they participate.

2. Set up and go public

After creating your club you land on the admin dashboard. Complete these steps before you unhide the club:

  1. Open Settings and fill in the description, contact email, location, and any membership rules.
  2. Add your management team on the Team page.
  3. Create your first few rides so new members have something to sign up for.
  4. When you are ready to go public, return to Settings and uncheck "Hide club from public listings" under Visibility, then click Save Settings.

You can hide the club again at any time — for example during an off-season hiatus — and unhide it when you are ready to resume.

3. Complete club settings

From your club page, open Admin, then Settings. Add a tagline, description, contact email, logo, banner image, social links, membership settings, safety guidelines, and club bylaws if you have them.

Keep the description short and practical: who you are, where you ride, what pace groups you offer, and how new riders should get started.

4. Add your management team

Open Admin, then Team. Add members by username or email and choose the right role:

  • Admin: manages settings, members, rides, and content.
  • Ride manager: creates and manages rides.
  • Content editor: updates news posts and club content.
  • Treasurer: views and exports member information.

5. Publish rides

Go to Admin, then Rides, and choose New Ride. Add the ride title, date, start time, meeting location, distance, pace, ride type, leader, route link, and notes.

Use clear titles and notes. Include regroup expectations, drop/no-drop information, parking details, and anything riders should bring.

6. Manage signups and attendance

Open a ride from the admin ride list to view the roster. You can see who signed up, manage waitlists, and record attendance after the ride.

If a ride needs to be canceled, edit the ride and mark it as canceled. Riders will see the cancellation on the ride page.

7. Keep members informed

Use News from the admin dashboard for club announcements, schedule updates, event reminders, and other short updates.

8. Handle paid club dues

If your club collects dues, open Admin, then Settings, and enable paid dues under membership settings. Choose the workflow that matches how much automation you want.

Phase 1 is the simplest setup. Paste the payment link your club owns, such as a Stripe, PayPal, or Square hosted checkout link. Riders pay through that link, then a club admin confirms payment from the Team page.

Phase 2 connects your club's Stripe account to Paceline. Club admins complete Stripe-hosted onboarding from club settings. Paceline then sends riders to Stripe Checkout for the dues amount you configure. Stripe deposits funds into your connected club account, and Paceline receives a payment-success notification that activates the rider automatically.

Paceline does not hold club dues or take a cut of this money in either workflow. The duration setting controls how long each confirmed or automatically paid membership stays active.

For Phase 2, riders who are waiting on dues see a Pay Club Dues button on the club page. After Stripe confirms the payment, Paceline marks the membership active automatically. If Stripe is not connected or the club uses Phase 1, the rider follows the external dues link and waits for a club admin to confirm payment from the Team page.

When importing an existing roster, add a paid-through date for the whole batch or add dates per row using rider@example.com, 2026-12-31.

9. Embed rides on your website

Every club gets an embeddable ride widget you can drop into any website — your club's WordPress site, Squarespace page, or any platform that accepts custom HTML.

The widget shows your next 30 days of upcoming rides. Each ride is a link that opens the full ride page on Paceline. The widget automatically picks up your club's logo and theme color.

To get your embed code:

  1. Open Admin → Settings on your club page.
  2. Scroll to the Embed Ride Widget section near the bottom.
  3. A live preview of the widget is shown. Click Copy to Clipboard to copy the embed code.
  4. Paste the <iframe> code anywhere in your website's HTML editor.

Example embed code: 

<iframe
  src="https://paceline.club/clubs/your-club/embed"
  width="100%"
  height="450"
  frameborder="0"
  scrolling="auto"
  title="Your Club — Upcoming Rides"
  style="border:none;border-radius:8px;display:block">
</iframe>

You can adjust the height value to fit more or fewer rides in the widget. The widget is responsive — it adapts to the width of its container.

Start simple: publish accurate club details and your next few rides first. You can add polish, leaders, sponsors, bylaws, and advanced settings later.
